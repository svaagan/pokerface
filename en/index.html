Pokerface

One of you doesn't know the word.

Pokerface is a party game for 3–20 players on one iPhone. Pass the phone around: everyone sees the same secret word – except the bluffer. Can you call out the bluffer before they figure out the word?

How to play

  1. Set up the roundAdd all the players and pick categories.
  2. Pass the phone aroundPress and hold to see your card in secret. Everyone gets the same word – except the bluffer.
  3. Take turns talkingEach say one thing about the word without saying it. The bluffer has to keep a poker face.
  4. Call out the blufferVote out loud, then tap “Reveal” for the answer. If the bluffer guesses the word, they still win.
